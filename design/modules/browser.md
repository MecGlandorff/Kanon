## Responsibility
A Chromium-family browser on the engineer's machine that opens a URL in app
mode: a window without tabs or an address bar. See the
[README](../../README.md#four-commands) for window-placement behavior.

## Interface
- in: the app flag on Chrome, Chromium, Edge, Brave, or Arc
- fallback: the platform's default open command
