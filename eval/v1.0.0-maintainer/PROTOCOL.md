# Kanon v1.0.0 solo-maintainer assurance standard

Status: **frozen; awaiting authentic solo-maintainer approval**

This is an additive governance lane for a human-owned risk decision about an
explicit-invocation-only, advisory, non-enforcing product. It is not release
evidence under the frozen prospective protocol.

## Separation from the evidence-strict lane

This standard:

- does not activate, amend, weaken, pass, or supersede
  `eval/v1.0.0-prospective/`;
- does not repair the historical
  `failed-required-comparison-unavailable` strict-equivalence result;
- creates no independent, blinded, causal, or unseen-holdout evidence;
- authorizes only a human-owned acceptance or rejection of named residual
  risks for this advisory product;
- does not make maintainer certification equivalent to a release-supported
  conclusion under the prospective protocol; and
- leaves the evidence-strict lane frozen, inactive, and available for future
  completion by the required real humans.

The six-person simulation is process assurance only. Its personas are not
humans, its labels are not independent human labels, and its successful
mechanical gates grant no release authority.

The only conclusions in this lane are:

- `maintainer-certification-ready`;
- `maintainer-certification-not-ready`; and
- `maintainer-certification-inconclusive`.

## Meaning of v1.0.0 in this lane

Here, v1.0.0 would be a stability and packaging commitment for an advisory
tool. It would not assert perfect ranking accuracy or independent validation.
The candidate product source remains commit
`7d35c81742ca7cbcd26207f3cf7b18fc09804041`; the governance-only commits in
this lane must leave the production artifact at
`0f87af4dfd851c268891c96237accf20b5089b0e0b95e10ac1417efebcca83a9`
and the package version at `0.4.0-rc.1`.

Public release notes must stay exactly within the following claim boundary.
They may report only:

- deterministic artifact construction;
- installed-artifact conformance;
- public API and capability stability;
- explicit-only invocation;
- advisory and non-enforcing behavior;
- zero runtime, optional, and peer dependencies;
- documented compatibility;
- complete passing project validation on platforms actually observed; and
- known development-corpus metrics, with their limitations.

They must not claim:

- an evidence-strict release-supported conclusion;
- independent validation or blinded human review;
- causal improvement or an official holdout score;
- historical strict-equivalence success;
- resolution of the seven visible performance-threshold failures;
- generalization beyond observed evidence;
- native-platform conformance that was not executed;
- correction-derived precision improvement; or
- any release, publication, tag, or version change that did not occur.

## Evidence classification

The machine-readable protocol binds every authority used by this lane.
Historical D.2 material is visible development evidence. The post-correction
evaluation is development-only diagnostic evidence. The six-person simulation
is simulated process assurance. Deterministic tests, packs, and installed
conformance are engineering assurance. Human independence and unseen holdout
evidence are absent. A release decision is awaiting an authentic human solo
maintainer.

Simulation evidence must never be copied into a human-evidence field.

## Risk policy

`RISK_LEDGER.json` is canonical. Each record retains a Known, Likely, or
Unknown classification and an open resolution status. Acceptance is not
resolution, and an Unknown does not become passing.

Evidence-independence and observed performance shortcomings may be accepted
only by the real solo maintainer, by exact risk identifier. A known P0
product-integrity, containment, security, data-loss, or packaging defect may
not be waived. Future maintenance obligations are also non-waivable. A failed
engineering gate may not be relabeled as passing.

## Certification gates

The machine-readable protocol contains the exhaustive gate list.
`maintainer-certification-ready` requires every mechanical and integrity gate
to pass, no unresolved non-waivable P0 defect, a complete ledger, no forbidden
claim, and a valid authentic-human waiver with a `proceed` decision accepting
every required waiver-eligible risk.

The documentation gate covers the root and packaged README, changelog,
installation, compatibility, security, and limitation statements. It is an
engineering gate and cannot be waived as an evidence or performance risk.

Any failed required gate yields
`maintainer-certification-not-ready`. Any genuinely Unknown required
engineering result yields `maintainer-certification-inconclusive`. If all
mechanical gates pass but the human waiver is unsigned, incomplete, invalid,
or declines, the conclusion remains
`maintainer-certification-not-ready`.

## Unsigned human waiver

`WAIVER.template.json` is intentionally unsigned with status
`awaiting-solo-maintainer-signature`. No agent may fill its human fields. The
real maintainer must later provide an accountable identity, UTC approval
timestamp, exact standard and ledger commitments, exact candidate source
commit, exact artifact hash, every accepted risk identifier, every required
acknowledgment, an out-of-band authenticity confirmation, and a `proceed` or
`decline` decision.

The validator rejects placeholder, agent/model, and simulated-persona
identities; incomplete or mismatched bindings; omitted risks or
acknowledgments; any attempt to call a failed gate passing; signing before the
standard and ledger are frozen; and any claim that a release action occurred.

## Post-release obligations

If a later, separately authorized certification and release occur, the
maintainer must:

- preserve all existing evidence and waivers;
- never describe the release as evidence-strict;
- document known accuracy limitations and a clear issue-reporting path;
- classify material ranking failures;
- maintain compatibility and security-response procedures;
- treat every newly observed repository as development-visible;
- never reuse a future holdout after exposure;
- keep migration to the evidence-strict protocol possible; and
- require a new risk decision for any material capability, enforcement,
  dependency, or behavioral expansion.

This standard promises no unavailable reviewers, timeline, or unexecuted
platform support. It authorizes no version bump, tag, publication, push, or
release action.
